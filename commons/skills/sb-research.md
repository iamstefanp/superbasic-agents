# SB Research — the eight phases

The SuperBasic™ research process. Carried by: Researcher, Strategist,
Reviewer.

Two modes, locked at BRIEF and never changed mid-run:
**LIGHT** — 3+ sources. **HEAVY** — 5+ sources, triangulation required.

---

**1 · BRIEF.** What decision does this answer serve? What would change
depending on the result? Lock the mode. No research without a Brief — a
question without one is a conversation, and answering it as though it were
research produces false confidence in an unscoped answer.

**2 · SCOPE.** What is in, what is out. What is the recency threshold — how
old can a source be before it is answering a different question? Fast-moving
fields decay in months.

**3 · PLAN.** Where would a *first source* for this live? A filing, a
primary document, the person themselves, the original announcement. Go there
before going where the search ranks.

**4 · INTEL.** Gather. Record what you searched — including searches that
returned nothing. Absence of evidence is a finding and it is the one most
often silently dropped.

**5 · CHECK.** Is this pool good enough for the locked mode? If not, return
to INTEL. Do not proceed thin and hedge at the end — that produces an answer
shaped like a finding but built on nothing.

**6 · VERIFY.** Score every source (see source-scoring). Apply SB-VERIFY.
Anything that fails is excluded, not softened. A Bronze claim never gets
presented as Gold.

**7 · SYNTHESIZE.** Produce an interpretation the sources support — not a
pile of quotes. Where sources disagree, surface the disagreement. A
contradiction found is worth more than a consensus manufactured.

**8 · REPORT.** Answer first. Evidence beneath it. Sources with tiers.
What was searched and not found. What remains open.

---

**Two loops back, both legitimate:** CHECK → INTEL when the pool is thin.
VERIFY → INTEL when sources fail the gate. Looping is the process working,
not the process failing.

**LIGHT is not "skip the phases."** It is the same eight phases with a lower
source floor. The Brief is never skipped, in any mode.
