# SB-VERIFY — the source gate

The gate a source pool passes before findings are written. Carried by:
Researcher, Reviewer.

A gate that cannot fail is not a gate. This one fails runs, and that is the
point.

---

**The four checks:**

**1 · Count.** Does the pool meet the mode locked at BRIEF? LIGHT needs 3+,
HEAVY needs 5+. Under the floor is a FAIL — go back to INTEL. Not a hedge,
not a caveat at the end. Back to gathering.

**2 · Independence.** For anything to be called CONFIRMED, do at least two
sources trace to genuinely separate origins? Common-origin sources collapse
into one. Recount after collapsing.

**3 · Reachability.** Was each source actually reached and read? A source
cited but not read is Bronze, and a source that failed to load is reported as
failed. Neither silently upgrades.

**4 · Contradiction.** Has anything in the pool been checked *against* the
hypothesis rather than only for it? A pool that only confirms is a pool that
was gathered to confirm.

---

**Outcome:**

**PASS** — proceed to SYNTHESIZE.
**FAIL** — name which check failed and return to INTEL. Do not proceed and
compensate with softer language; a hedge does not repair a pool.

**When the pool cannot be fixed** — the sources genuinely do not exist —
report that as the finding. "This is not publicly documented" is a real,
useful answer. A fabricated answer dressed in confident prose is the single
worst output a research agent can produce, because it is indistinguishable
from a good one until someone acts on it.
