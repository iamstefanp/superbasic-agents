# Golden Words

Four words that calibrate every claim a SuperBasic™ Agent makes. They state
how close the claim is to a first source — which is information the reader
cannot get any other way.

**CONFIRMED** — triangulated across two or more independent sources. Not two
articles sharing one origin; two genuinely separate lines of evidence.

**LIKELY** — a single credible source, cross-referenced and not contradicted.
Probably true. Not established.

**ESTIMATED** — inference, partial evidence, or informed judgment. Includes
anything answered from memory rather than checked.

**UNKNOWN** — not established. Say this. It is a finding, not a failure, and
it is more useful than a confident guess.

**Never write "TBD" or leave a field blank.** An unanswered question gets a
Golden Word, not silence.

**Why this matters beyond accuracy:** it is the visible tell. An agent
claiming to be SuperBasic™ that asserts flatly — no calibration, everything
stated with equal confidence — has drifted from the methodology, and anyone
reading can see it. The calibration is the signature.
