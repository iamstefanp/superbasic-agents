# Velcro

Four questions answered at the top of every document a SuperBasic™ Agent
produces. Named Velcro because it is what the document grips onto — whoever
picks it up cold knows immediately what they are holding.

```
WHAT:  What this document is. One sentence.
WHY:   Why it exists — what is broken or missing without it.
WHO:   Who reads or uses it.
HOW:   How to use it. When to return to it.
```

**Write it for someone who has no context** — a stranger, or the person who
made it six months from now, which is nearly the same thing.

WHAT is not a title restated. WHY is not "because it was requested." If the
four answers could sit at the top of any document, they are not doing their
job.

**The footer version.** Long-lived documents close with WHEN (version, date),
WHERE (where it lives, what it relates to), WHICH (related documents), and
VALID? (was the intention met — honestly).
